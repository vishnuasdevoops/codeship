│ 01. LAYOUT, DISPLAY & POSITIONING                                            │

  │   ├── display          : block | inline | inline-block | flex | inline-flex |
  │   │                      grid | inline-grid | flow-root | contents | none
  │   ├── visibility       : visible | hidden | collapse

  │   ├── position         : static | relative | absolute | fixed | sticky
